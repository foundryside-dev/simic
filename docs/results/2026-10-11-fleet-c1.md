- **`mild` (the honest, non-designed host).** Uniform 1.25× scale-up beat the graft by 0.012

- **How to read `c1`.** The `c1` string is computed from `under_normalized` alone. PDR-0057

  to designed pathologies, the expected result". So C1 holds at bounded scale on a designed
  pathology, and this fleet does not show it on the non-designed host.

## G1: graft against uniform scale-up

Graft − scale-up in late dev CE: negative means the graft did better. The rules:
- **Passing a step:** the upper bounds of both the paired mean (t) and the 10%-trimmed mean
  (bootstrap, 20,000 resamples) must be below the 0.02 margin. The bounds are one-sided at
  0.0125, the family α of 0.025 split over the two co-primary hosts.
- **Step-down:** steps run 1.25× → 1.5× → 2×. The step-down stops at the first step that
  fails, or at a comparator that diverges in more than 2.5% of runs. No comparator diverged.
- **`inferior`:** both lower bounds above +0.02 at the first step.

| Host | Role | m | Mean (upper) | Trimmed mean (upper) | Step | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `under_normalized` | co-primary | 1.25× | −0.080 (−0.071) | −0.082 (−0.074) | pass | **non-inferior to 2×** |
| | | 1.5× | −0.076 (−0.066) | −0.075 (−0.067) | pass | |
| | | 2× | −0.071 (−0.061) | −0.072 (−0.063) | pass | |
| `mild` | co-primary | 1.25× | +0.012 (+0.0201) | +0.011 (+0.0185) | fail | **inconclusive** |
| `channel_starved` | exploratory | 1.25× | +0.010 (+0.018) | +0.009 (+0.017) | pass | non-inferior to 1.25×, but its graft is larger than that comparator (below) |
| | | 1.5× | +0.014 (+0.022) | +0.013 (+0.021) | fail | |
| `no_spatial_mix` | exploratory | 1.25× | +0.002 (+0.008) | +0.001 (+0.008) | pass | non-inferior to 1.5× |

| | | 2× | +0.018 (+0.025) | +0.017 (+0.024) | fail | |

**`under_normalized`.** Every upper bound is below zero as well as below the margin. So the graft
is ahead at each step, not just within the margin.

**`mild`.** The 1.25× scale-up is 1.260× the host's parameters and the graft is 1.063×. The
scale-up was ahead by 0.012 (trimmed 0.011), with one-sided lower bounds 0.0037 and 0.0028,
both above zero. The upper bounds are 0.02009 for the mean, just above the 0.02 margin, and
0.0185 for the trimmed mean. The rule needs both below the margin for non-inferiority and both
lower bounds above it for `inferior`, so the reading is `inconclusive`. The pre-registered rule
stands.

  The one-sided direction was chosen after seeing 0, so both p-values are given.
  - **Like for like, rung 4 at 10 epochs: 3/768.** Rung 4 ran the same config and arm. Fisher
    p = 0.125 one-sided, 0.25 two-sided, so the two fleets agree.
  - **All five earlier studies at 10 epochs: 6/984,** the diagnosis's stated prior. p = 0.031
    one-sided, 0.038 two-sided. The difference comes from the four small studies (3/216; p =
    0.010 against Fleet C1). Rung 4 and those four do not differ significantly from each other

    step, it caught the graft divergence above on GPU, and unit verification rejects a
    non-finite late CE. Its bit-for-bit reproduction of rung 4 was checked on seeds that did
    not diverge; the divergence path itself was tested on CPU.
  - **Pooled 10-epoch rate,** including Fleet C1: 6/1,752 = 0.34% (0.13–0.74%).
- No other arm on any host diverged.

## What this does not say

- **Bounded scale.** Hosts of 117k–161k parameters before scale-up, 4,096 fit examples,
  10 epochs, one site. The reading's name says so. Nothing here extends past 2× or to larger
  hosts.
- **Designed fixes.** On `under_normalized`, `channel_starved` and `no_spatial_mix`, the graft
  is the seed designed for that host's built-in pathology. So C1 asks whether the right fix,
  targeted, beats spending parameters uniformly, and it does not test choosing the fix. `mild`
  has no designed pathology, and that is where the graft did not pass.
- **The comparator keeps the pathology.** Scale-up widens every stage of the same host. On
  `under_normalized` the contrast is therefore a norm seed against a wider network that still
  has no normalisation.
- **Growing versus installing (D4).** A G1 pass shows that a targeted graft beats uniform
  width. It does not show that growing during training beats installing at the start: static is
  0.046 ahead on `under_normalized`.
- **One fixed dataset.** All 768 seeds share one pinned fit set and one dev set, checked per
  unit. The earlier studies that shaped these hosts used the same data seed. So the intervals
  cover seed variation on that single draw. They do not cover a fresh data sample, and the dev
  set is a reused development set.
- **Dev CE, not test.** Outer/test data was not read.
