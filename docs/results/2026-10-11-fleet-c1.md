## How big the graft is

The plan's question calls the graft "about 1.0× parameters". The realised sizes
([E1](2026-10-11-fleet-c1/extras.md)) are:

| Host | Designed seed | Graft | 1.25× arm | 1.5× arm | 2× arm |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `under_normalized` | `norm` | 1.001 | 1.244 | 1.482 | 1.984 |
| `no_spatial_mix` | `attn` | 1.037 | 1.229 | 1.460 | 1.990 |
| `mild` | `conv_light` | 1.063 | 1.260 | 1.517 | 1.985 |
| `channel_starved` | `conv_heavy` | **1.463** | 1.241 | 1.530 | 2.014 |

All are multiples of the host's no-growth parameters.

The two co-primary readings compare a graft of 1.001× and 1.063× against larger hosts.
`channel_starved`'s graft is larger than its 1.25× comparator and close to its 1.5× one. So
its "non-inferior to 1.25×" compares a bigger model with a smaller one, and it is no evidence
that the graft is worth more than its own parameters there. That reading is exploratory, and
no pre-registered reading depends on it. I found no statement of these multiples in the plan,
the pilot note or the pre-flight.

## Divergences

- **One graft divergence** in 3,072 graft runs ([E2](2026-10-11-fleet-c1/extras.md)): seed 10203,
  `under_normalized`, epoch 3 step 74, while blending. The training objective went non-finite,
  and the gain witness reached −1.6×10⁸. That is 1/768 on that host, within the 2.5% cap. It is
  scored at ln 10, as registered, and not diagnosed here.
- **Static on `under_normalized`: 0/768.** All 768 completed; the worst late CE was 2.013,
  against ln 10 = 2.303 for chance ([E3](2026-10-11-fleet-c1/extras.md)). The
  [static-divergence diagnosis](2026-10-11-static-norm-divergence.md) counted:
  - **Rung 4 at 10 epochs, 3/768.** Rung 4 has the same config and the same arm (the atlas
    reproduces its static records bit for bit). Seeing 0 at its rate has probability 0.049;
    one-sided Fisher p = 0.125.
  - **All five earlier studies at 10 epochs, 6/984.** Probability 0.009; Fisher p = 0.031.
  - So there is a weak hint of a lower rate, and no sign that the arm differs. The pooled
    10-epoch rate, including Fleet C1, is 6/1,752 = 0.34% (0.13–0.74%).
- No other arm on any host diverged.

## What this does not say

- **Bounded scale.** Hosts of 117k–161k parameters before scale-up, 4,096 fit examples, 10 epochs
  and one site.
  The reading's name says so. Nothing here extends past 2× or to larger hosts.
- **The graft is the designed fix.** Each host's seed is the one matched to its pathology by
  design. C1 asks whether the right fix, targeted, beats spending parameters uniformly. It
  does not test choosing the fix.
- **Dev CE, not test.** Outer/test data was not read.
