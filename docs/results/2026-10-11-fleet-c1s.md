## Validity

- **Pairing.** On all 48 pairing seeds, the re-trained arms equal Fleet C1's records bit for
  bit: 0 mismatches. No fallback condition occurred: Fleet C1's instrument was `ok`, and no
  pairing, identity or failed-unit limit was hit.
- **Birth.** Every one of the 2,352 corrected arms was born with a realised ratio of 0.0500
  (the plan requires within 5% of τ = 0.05). Fleet C1's registered arm was born at about
  0.0013–0.0018 on these hosts, according to the pilot's witnesses.
- **Divergence.** The plan worried that a 30× larger birth might make static diverge more
  often. The corrected arm diverged in 0 of 2,304 runs on the BatchNorm hosts.
