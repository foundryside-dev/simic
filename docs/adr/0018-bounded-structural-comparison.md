## Context

Inspection found a functioning experimental substrate in `experiments/kernel_demo.py`,
not an empty project. The August smoke produced preflight evidence but did not
freeze or proceed to collection, policy training or evaluation. The source package
remains a scaffold. The larger design and its historical decisions are retained.

John authorized the smallest credible Esper-lite reboot under the existing Simic
name, with an independent review and recoverable preservation of existing work.
The predecessor assessment found that reward/measurement coupling, missing evidence
and an inadequate capacity comparator undermine interpretation even when the
mechanics work. More controller machinery cannot repair those information flows.

## Decision

Add an experimental comparison using the existing mild CNN and one `conv_light`
residual seed. Compare no added capacity, that same extra capacity trained normally
from the start, and a prescribed graft using Simic's existing lifecycle. Reuse the
training primitives; retain the old campaign unchanged. The authoritative runnable
contract is [the bounded comparison guide](../bounded-comparison.md).

Keep training and terminal outer evaluation separate. Freeze configuration and
selection rules before evaluation, pair initialization and training futures,
record actual optimizer ownership and work, and make incomplete evidence a failure.
Require measured gradients, parameter updates and learning on an independently
generated holdout fixture. A smoke is engineering evidence, not a CIFAR result.

Simic's residual STE training, alpha blending, beta recoupling and continued seed
training are deliberate semantics here. They are not Esper-lite's convex blend,
detached prefix and frozen fossil. The static comparator tests the whole scheduled
lifecycle against upfront capacity; it does not isolate timing from calibration,
optimization and coupling effects.

## Displaced constraints

None constitutional. This is an explicitly bounded experiment, like the existing
kernel demo, not an implementation or certification of the full HLD authority
system. It does not claim warrants, generated structure, policy learning or complete
compliance with every production invariant. No locked names or subsystem authorities
change. The user-authorized immediate sequence takes precedence over the older
Phase A programme for this experimental work.

## Consequences

The first research question is whether this structural intervention earns its cost
against both controls. Random, heuristic and learned timing follow only if that
question warrants more work. The eventual telemetry-conditioned, cross-model learned
structural adaptation ambition—called Tamiyo in the Esper-lite discussion—remains
visible, but is not a result of this tranche. Simic's existing Namespec meaning of
Tamiyo remains unchanged.

No GPU campaign, multi-seed screen, dataset download, paid service or remote
publication is authorized by this decision. Benchmark and propose a bounded next
experiment with its budget before such execution. Reopen the design if the static
control wins at the declared cost, or if measurement uncertainty prevents a credible
comparison; do not respond merely by enlarging the controller.
