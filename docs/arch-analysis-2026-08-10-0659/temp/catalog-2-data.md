## Data, Episodes & Telemetry

**Location:** `experiments/kernel_demo.py` (lines 252–554, 990–1141)

**Responsibility:** Supplies the deterministic CIFAR-10 split, the pre-drawn shared augmentation schedule that makes compared arms matched, the blind per-epoch telemetry record and its feature vector, and the single-epoch training/evaluation step that produces them.

**Key Components:**
- `DataBundle` (line 252) - Flat six-tensor carrier for train/val/test `x`/`y`; all six are moved to the compute device in `load_data` (lines 296–303), including the 10,000-image test set, regardless of whether the caller intends to read it.
- `split_indices` (line 262) - Derives the 45,000/5,000 train/val partition as `torch.randperm(50_000)` under `make_generator(derive(run_seed, "valsplit"))` (line 263). Fully deterministic from `cfg.run_seed`; disjoint by construction as a partition of one permutation. Test is the official CIFAR-10 test split (`train=False`, line 284), never drawn from the 50,000. Selftest `partition_check` (lines 2224–2231) independently asserts sizes 45k/5k and `torch.isin` non-overlap. **No leakage between the three splits.**
- `data_split_id` (line 268) - SHA-256 over length-prefixed `train_idx`/`val_idx` index bytes (lines 271–275). Identifies *which* partition, not *what data*. Consumed by `freeze_manifest` (line 2883) and enforced as a replay refusal (line 3955).
- `load_data` (line 279) - Deferred `import torchvision` (line 280) so the module imports without it; `download=True` against hardcoded `root = "runs/data"` (lines 282–284). `subset` truncates `train_idx` only (lines 288–289), leaving val and test at full size.
- `CommonFuture` (line 308) + `draw` (line 316) - **Common random numbers, verified by construction, not by name.** All per-step stochasticity for a whole episode is pre-drawn up front from one generator: batch `order` per epoch, `crops` in `[0,9)`, and `flips` (lines 322–324), plus a SHA-256 `hash` (lines 325–328). `make_episode` draws exactly one instance (line 1020) and the *same object* is threaded to every arm — `run_fan` passes it to the noop arm (line 1381), each seed arm (line 1392) and the nullseed arm (line 1402), received as the `future` parameter of `run_arm` (line 1291). Arms therefore see byte-identical data order and augmentation. `run_refan` deliberately draws a *different* future under a distinct label tuple (line 2469) to supply the gate-3 noise floor. `draw` refuses a subset too small to form one step (lines 320–321).
- `augment` (line 332) - Normalize → reflect-pad 4 → gather the crop window by advanced indexing → conditional horizontal flip (lines 334–343). Consumes the pre-drawn `crops`/`flips` slices; draws no randomness itself.
- `TelemetryDivergence` (line 347) - Raised only from `TelemetryRecord.__post_init__` (lines 375, 379) when any float field is NaN or ±inf. It guards the *record construction boundary*, so a numerically diverged epoch cannot enter telemetry, the store, or the policy's feature stream. Caught by the fan executor (line 1256), `run_refan` (line 2437) and `run_eval` (lines 3518, 3583), which convert it into a recorded void/diverged outcome rather than a crash. Registered `_NON_SEMANTIC` (line 504) so its source is excluded from `config_hash`.
- `TelemetryRecord` (line 357) - **Blinding is by ABSENCE of fields, not by ignoring them.** Field-by-field, lines 358–369 are: `epoch`, `train_loss`, `val_loss`, `val_acc`, two loss deltas, and four per-stage triples (`grad_norm_mean`, `grad_norm_var`, `act_saturation`, `weight_norm`), `per_class_val_acc_std`, `confusion_entropy`. There is **no** seed name, arm name, pathology id, episode seed, or provenance field anywhere in the dataclass — the identity is not present to be ignored. `frozen=True` prevents post-construction mutation.
- `record_to_vector` (line 397) - Maps exactly those 20 fields to a `TELEMETRY_DIM`-20 float32 vector (lines 398–420). No identity or provenance enters the feature vector; `epoch` (index 0, `EPOCH_FEATURE_IDX` line 352) is the only non-metric feature and carries no arm/seed information. **No leakage of identity into features.**
- `confusion_stats` (line 424) - Builds a 10×10 confusion matrix by explicit double loop (lines 428–434) and returns two scalars: the standard deviation of per-class accuracy (line 438) and the Shannon entropy of the normalized *off-diagonal* (error) mass (lines 439–446), with an explicit `0.0` when there are no errors.
- `Normalizer` (line 451) - Median/IQR feature scaler. Default construction is the identity transform (zeros/ones, lines 456–463); `fit` computes per-dimension median and clamped IQR (lines 465–471). **Fit on preflight material only, then frozen.** The single `fit` call site is `run_preflight` (lines 2958–2961) over telemetry of `seed_namespace == "preflight"` fans only (line 2957); the result is serialized into `frozen.json` (line 2878) and thereafter loaded read-only via `from_json` by `run_train` (line 3347) and `run_eval` (line 3493). Preflight episodes run `read_test=False` (line 2941), and telemetry contains only train/val-derived quantities, so **the normalizer never sees test material and is frozen before any training or evaluation** — no calibration-on-test.
- `build_record` (line 508) - Keyword-only pass-through constructor (lines 522–535). Adds no fields, so it cannot widen the blind surface; it also performs no numeric coercion, delegating all validation to `__post_init__`.
- `normalize_u8` (line 990) - Eval-path normalization: same mean/std as `augment`, no crop/flip (lines 991–994).
- `EpisodeCtx` (line 999) - Episode state: config, the shared `DataBundle`, device, `episode_seed`, derived `pathology`, the `CommonFuture`, live `host`/`opt`/`slot`, the accumulating `telemetry` list, `curves_val`, and `curves_test` typed `list[float] | None` — `None` iff `read_test=False`, an explicit convention against empty-list ambiguity (line 1011).
- `make_episode` (line 1016) - Derives everything from `episode_seed` via labelled derivation: pathology (line 1017), host init (line 1018), and the future (line 1020). `read_test` defaults to `False` (line 1016).
- `evaluate_acc` (line 1041) - Chunked accuracy under `no_grad`; captures and restores the prior `training` flag for *both* host and slot (lines 1042, 1052–1053).
- `train_one_epoch` (line 1058) - The episode's unit of work. **Draws no RNG:** batch order, crops and flips are all indexed out of `ctx.future` (lines 1060, 1069), so the function issues no new random draws — the global stream still matters for any module-internal stochastic op, which is why `Snapshot` carries `cpu_rng` (line 1149). Per-step it augments, forwards, adds `slot.trust_region_loss`, backprops, accumulates per-stage gradient norms (lines 1076–1081), steps, and ticks the slot. It then evaluates the full val split under `no_grad` (lines 1094–1106), assembles the record (lines 1119–1132) and appends to `telemetry`/`curves_val`.

**Patterns Observed:**
- **Pre-drawn futures over live sampling.** Every stochastic choice in a training epoch is materialized before the epoch runs (lines 322–324) and consumed by index (lines 1060, 1069). This is what makes matched arms matched and what lets `train_one_epoch` be RNG-free.
- **Blinding by schema absence.** The blind view is the only view — `TelemetryRecord` (lines 358–369) has no provenance fields to strip, so no consumer can accidentally read one.
- **Typed failure at the construction boundary.** `TelemetryDivergence` fires inside `__post_init__` (lines 371–393), so a non-finite epoch cannot become a record; callers convert it to a recorded outcome (lines 1256, 2437, 3518) rather than a traceback.
- **Absence encoded structurally, not sentinel-wise.** `curves_test: list[float] | None` with the invariant stated in-line (line 1011); `r_test` is `None` rather than a magic number when unobserved (line 1342).
- **Hash-bound identity threaded through.** `CommonFuture.hash` (line 328) and `data_split_id` (line 275) become replay refusal anchors (lines 3955, 3971).
- **Mode-restoring evaluation.** Both eval paths capture and restore prior `training` flags (lines 1042/1052–1053, 1091/1107–1108) rather than assuming a mode.

**Concerns:**
- **`record_to_vector` (line 397) and `_telemetry_vector_from_dict` (line 1811) are a coupled pair with no equality check.** They must emit the same 20 values in the same order, and that contract is enforced only by a comment (line 1812). The live path uses `record_to_vector` (line 1749) while the stored path uses the dict twin (lines 1843, 2959, 3386) — the *same* policy and the *same* normalizer consume both. Verified by grep: `record_to_vector` appears only at its definition and line 1749, and nothing anywhere asserts the two agree; selftest checks the data partition (line 2224) but not this. A field reordering in one would silently desynchronize the normalizer's calibration and the policy's training features from its inference features, with no gate firing. **Latent, not live:** `record_to_vector` reaches the model only via `_policy_tokens` (line 1747), whose sole callers are `decide_live` (line 1766) and `query_teacher_forced` (line 1789) — both currently dead, so fit and inference cannot desynchronize today. The mechanism claim stands and the coupling is unguarded; only its present reachability is nil. Ranked first because it becomes live the moment either caller is wired up, and nothing would announce that.
- **Dataset *content* is not bound to run identity.** `data_split_id` (line 268) hashes index bytes only, and `freeze_manifest` records `data_split_id` plus `n_train` (lines 2883–2886) — there is no checksum of the pixel data. All refusal gates check only those two: collect (line 3088), eval (line 3465), replay (lines 3955, 3964). Mechanism: `load_data` fetches with `download=True` into a hardcoded `runs/data` (lines 282–284), where torchvision's integrity check applies to the downloaded archive, not to an already-extracted tree. A modified-but-same-size CIFAR-10 extraction passes every gate and replays "clean". In a pipeline whose thesis is that a result can be traced to the code and configuration that produced it, the data is the one input not covered.
- **`check_finite` silently no-ops on unrecognized types** (lines 372–381): `float` and `tuple` are validated, `int` explicitly passes, and there is no `else: raise`. A 0-dim `torch.Tensor` NaN would pass the guard and then survive `torch.tensor(values)` in `record_to_vector`. Latent, not live — every current call site coerces through `float(...)` (lines 1084, 1101, 1105, 1112, 1117) and `build_record` (line 508) is not called from outside `train_one_epoch`. Reported because it is the silent-default shape in a module that elsewhere states the opposite contract explicitly ("KeyError = hooks never attached — loud, never silent zeros", line 1085).
- **`confusion_stats` biases `per_class_val_acc_std` when a class is absent** (lines 429–437): a class with no samples leaves its confusion row all-zero, `total_per_class` is clamped to `1e-8`, and its accuracy resolves to `0.0` rather than being excluded — inflating the standard deviation. Immaterial on the 5,000-sample val split where all ten classes are present, but live for `_tiny_bundle_for_selftest` (line 2036), where the affected feature feeds a telemetry vector.
- **Test-split access is gated by convention at the tensor boundary and by enforcement at the record boundary — two different mechanisms.** `read_test` is a plain boolean defaulting to `False` (line 1016), and test tensors are read only under `if ctx.read_test:` (lines 1135–1137). Collection and preflight pass `False` (line 2941); only `run_eval` (lines 3512, 3542, 3646) and `--replay` (line 3966, itself derived from the record's own `r_test` presence, lines 3959–3962) pass `True`. But `test_x`/`test_y` sit in the *same* `DataBundle` (lines 257–258) handed to every episode (line 1001), on-device, so nothing structural prevents a future code path from reading them — there is no type, no accessor, and no exception at the data layer. `SplitViolation` (line 1420) is not that guard: it enforces record `split_role` on the training path (lines 1667–1669), downstream in Records & Store.
- **Minor:** `CommonFuture.draw` hashes its three tensors without the length prefixing that `data_split_id` uses (lines 325–328 vs 271–274). Unreachable as a collision given fixed shapes, but it is an inconsistency in canonicalisation discipline between two hashes that both serve as replay anchors.
