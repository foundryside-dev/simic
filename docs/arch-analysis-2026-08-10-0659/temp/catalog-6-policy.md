## Policy & Learning

**Location:** `experiments/kernel_demo.py` (lines 1682–1978) — author's sections 11 (POLICY, `:1680`) and 12 (LEARNING, `:1800`)

**Responsibility:** Defines the factored decision network (a Bernoulli WHEN gate plus a 4-way WHICH distribution over seeds), the deterministic deployment rule, and the frozen-temperature expected-reward objective that trains the network from fully-observed counterfactual-fan reward vectors on the train/tune splits only.

**Key Components:**
- `_PolicyBlock` (line 1682) - Hand-rolled pre-LN transformer block: explicit-matmul single-head self-attention at attention-dim = `d_model`, bias-free q/k/v/proj (`:1689–1692`), causal mask applied by `masked_fill(..., -inf)` before softmax (`:1700–1701`), MLP ratio 4 with GELU (`:1694`). No `nn.MultiheadAttention`, no SDPA, no dropout — deliberate, so the arithmetic is inspectable and hashable.
- `Policy` (line 1708) - The **trunk** is `embed` (Linear 20→d, GELU, Linear d→d, `:1716`) + learned positional parameter `pos[horizon, d]` scaled 0.02 (`:1717`) + `n_layers` `_PolicyBlock`s (`:1718`) + final LayerNorm (`:1719`), read out at the last valid token via `x[arange(b), lengths-1]` (`:1730`). The **factored head** is two named submodules that together form the conceptual `Linear(d_model, 5)`: `now_head: Linear(d,1)` → scalar WHEN/act logit (`:1720`, `:1731`) and `seed_head: Linear(d,4)` → WHICH logits over `SEED_NAMES` (`:1721`, `:1732`). They are separate modules for test addressability (`:1709–1711`; `tests/unit/kernel_demo/test_learning.py:57,68` grabs each head's parameters directly). **The policy outputs both**: a when-to-act Bernoulli AND a distribution over the 4 seeds. Init happens inside `rng_scope(gen)` (`:1715`) so weights are seed-derived.
- `schedule_only_mask` (line 1737) - The ablation comparator's mask. Builds `zeros_like(t)` and copies back only `[..., EPOCH_FEATURE_IDX]` (`:1741–1742`) — i.e. **masking is by ZEROING in normalized space, on the FEATURE axis**, not by `-inf` and not by field absence. 19 of 20 telemetry features are erased; only the clock survives. The comment (`:1738–1740`) records a fixed defect where a dim-0 implementation zeroed the batch axis instead. Pinned 1-D and `[B,T,D]` by `tests/unit/kernel_demo/test_policy.py:47–56`.
- `_policy_tokens` (line 1747) - `record_to_vector` → `normalizer.apply` → stack → `[1,T,20]` on the policy's device (`:1748–1750`). Normalize-then-mask ordering is fixed here and re-asserted at `:1766–1768` and `:1895`.
- `decide_live` (line 1754) - The declared deployment rule: return `(False, None)` outside the inclusive window `cfg.window` (`:1762–1765`), else normalize → mask → forward under `no_grad` with train-mode save/restore (`:1769–1773`), **raise `RuntimeError` on non-finite logits** rather than let `NaN > 0.5 == False` read as "never germinate, lift 0" (`:1774–1777`), fire iff `sigmoid(p) > 0.5` (`:1778`), pick `argmax` with lowest-index tie-break (`:1780`).
- `query_teacher_forced` (line 1786) - Non-firing read of `(p, {seed: π})` at a telemetry prefix's last epoch (`:1789–1797`). No window guard, no mask, no finiteness check — it measures, it does not decide.
- `_as_float` (line 1802) - **Silent-default audit: CLEAN.** Rejects `bool` explicitly before the numeric check (`:1805` — `bool` is an `int` subclass, correctly excluded) and raises `ValueError` on anything non-`(int, float)`, including `None` (a serialized non-finite). There is no default arm and no `.get()`.
- `_telemetry_vector_from_dict` (line 1811) - Decoded-dict twin of `record_to_vector` (`:397`). **Silent-default audit: CLEAN.** `f(key)` uses direct subscript `d[key]` (`:1814`), so a **missing key raises `KeyError`**, never 0.0; a `None`/string/bool value raises `ValueError` via `_as_float`; `f3` validates list-or-tuple *and* `len == 3` before conversion (`:1816–1820`). Builds exactly 20 fields in `record_to_vector` order (`:1822–1836`).
- `fan_to_example` (line 1840) - Fan record → training example `{tokens, length, r, r_noop}` (`:1847–1852`). **The label is the FULL 4-element reward vector**, not an argmax class and not a regret: `r[i] = by_name[SEED_NAMES[i]]["r_val"]` (`:1846`), plus the scalar `r_noop = by_name["noop"]["r_val"]` (`:1851`). **The no-op arm is NOT a fifth class** in `seed_head` (which is `Linear(d,4)`, `:1721`) — it enters only through the WHEN head's mixture at `:1904`. Rewards are **`r_val` only**; `r_test` never reaches a training example. Diverged arms carry `cfg.diverged_r = 0.10` from `run_arm` rather than being dropped (`:1841–1842`).
- `measure_fan_density` (line 1856) - Averages two reward *gaps* over records: `best_minus_second` = best seed arm minus runner-up (`:1865`) and `best_minus_noop` = best seed arm minus the no-op arm (`:1866`). These are the natural scales of the two decisions the factored head makes, and they set the two entropy temperatures. `unit="val"` is the **split selector**: `key = {"val": "r_val", "test": "r_test"}[unit]` (`:1859`), so `"val"` reads validation-split rewards and an unknown unit raises `KeyError`. Loud on empty input (`:1857–1858`, pinned by `test_learning.py:120`).
- `warmup_schedule` (line 1874) - Pure predicate `step >= round(total_steps * warmup_frac)` (`:1876`); `False` during warm-up disables `J_now` for the first 30% of steps (`cfg.warmup_frac = 0.3`, `:173`). Boundaries pinned by `test_learning.py:43–47`.
- `policy_loss` (line 1880) - **The objective is direct expected reward under full feedback, not cross-entropy and not a policy gradient.** Because the fan observes every arm, `J_which = Σ π_i r_i` is computed in closed form and differentiated exactly (`:1897–1898`) — no sampling, no REINFORCE, no baseline. Entropy bonuses on both heads use `clamp_min(1e-8)` before `log` (`:1899`, `:1905`). The WHEN term mixes `J_now = p·adv_mix + (1-p)·r_noop` where `adv_mix` uses `pi.detach()` (`:1903–1904`) — a **stop-gradient that prevents the WHEN head backpropagating into WHICH**, pinned by `test_learning.py:50–60`. Both temperatures are separate explicit keyword args (`:1885–1886`) because they map from two different density keys (`:1890–1892`).
- `_collate_examples` (line 1911) - Right-pads to batch-max `T` with zeros in normalized space (`:1915`, `:1920`) and records true `lengths` (`:1921`), which the trunk's last-valid-index readout consumes.
- `train_policy` (line 1928) - `_assert_trainable` first (`:1938`, raising `SplitViolation` on any eval-role or non-`fan` record). **Temperatures are frozen, not fitted**: `beta_which = cfg.beta_which_frac * frozen_density["best_minus_second"]` and `beta_now = frozen_density["best_minus_noop"] / cfg.beta_now_div` (`:1941–1942`), where `frozen_density` is a **required keyword-only parameter with no default** (`:1934`) — recomputation from `records` is impossible by signature. Split roles are read off the records, never recomputed (`:1944–1946`). Adam at `cfg.policy_lr` (`:1950`). **Checkpoint selection is on the TUNE split** — `tune_batch` is collated once from `tune_ex` (`:1955`) and scored every `total_steps // 10` steps under `no_grad`, keeping the lowest-scoring state dict (`:1964–1974`).

Edges are labelled `[call]` or `[serialized-shape]`; the `serialized-shape` edges are precisely those mypy does not guard and therefore the ones that fail silently.

**Patterns Observed:**
- **Pre-registration chain for every fitted quantity, verified end to end.** The `Normalizer` is fit at preflight on preflight-namespace fans only (`:2956–2961`) and frozen into `frozen.json`; the density that sets both temperatures is measured on the same preflight fans (`:2963`, `:2880–2881`); `run_train` reads both back read-only (`:3347–3348`), re-derives the betas and *asserts* they match the manifest (`:3353–3354`), prints the train-set recomputation explicitly labelled "UNUSED for betas" (`:3352`), and refuses to run at all if the live config or source hash differs from the manifest (`:3343–3344`).
- **No train/eval leakage on this path — checked against every channel and found clean.** Training labels are `r_val` only (`:1846`, `:1851`); `r_test` appears solely on report and eval paths (`:3409`, `:3671`, `:3751`). Records reach training only through the train/tune filter (`:1674`) behind a guard placed on the yield path rather than vacuously behind the filter (`:1675–1676`), re-applied inside `train_policy` (`:1938`). **Checkpoint selection is on the tune split, not the evaluation split** (`:1955`, `:1964–1972`) — and eval-role records cannot physically reach the selector, since `_assert_trainable` raises on them first (`:1666`). `trained` and `schedule_only` are comparators reported side by side, not a best-of-two selected on test data (`:3358`).
- **The learning path honours the store's train/tune wall and never re-derives a split of its own** (cross-checked against explorer 5's storage-layer finding). `train_policy` partitions by reading the recorded field verbatim — `if r.split_role == "train"` / `== "tune"` (`:1945–1946`), under the standing comment "Records arrive pre-split into train/tune roles; never re-split" (`:1944`). There is no internal holdout, no `train_test_split`, no fraction, and no re-derivation of `train_tune_split` (`:1540`) anywhere in the range. Checkpoint selection reads **only** tune-role records: `tune_batch` is collated from `tune_ex` alone (`:1955`) and is the sole input to the scoring call (`:1966–1968`). Minibatch sampling cannot cross the wall by construction — `torch.randint(len(train_ex), ...)` (`:1957`) draws indices into the train list only, so the tune examples are outside the index space, and the draw is generator-seeded rather than globally shuffled. `_collate_examples` (`:1911–1924`) is pure padding and stacking: it preserves the order it is handed, and performs no shuffling, grouping, re-weighting, or cross-example mixing.
- **Episode identity is dropped at the training boundary, which enforces the wall but also forecloses episode-level grouping downstream.** Each episode yields exactly `cfg.fans_per_episode = 2` fans (`:153`, `:2340`, `:2348`), all stamped with one `split_role` computed once per episode before any fan exists (`:2339`), so a base trajectory cannot straddle the wall. But `fan_to_example` (`:1840`) returns only `{tokens, length, r, r_noop}` (`:1847–1852`) — `episode_seed` is not carried through. The learning path is therefore structurally incapable of grouping or clustering by episode, and its units are fans, not trajectories. Two bounded consequences, neither a wall violation and neither an inference claim: minibatches may contain both fans of one episode as if independent (harmless for SGD), and the full-batch tune score (`:1955`, `:1967`, mean at `:1907`) weights an episode by its surviving fan count — 2 normally, 1 or 0 where the base diverged and a snapshot was skipped (`:2352–2353`). The effect on checkpoint selection is small and bounded by that 2:1 ratio, but any future work wanting episode-clustered training or selection must re-add the identity field.
- **Loud-on-absence discipline throughout the decode boundary**, the project's silent-zero scar taken seriously: direct subscript rather than `.get()` (`:1814`), bool rejected as a number (`:1805`), 3-vector arity validated (`:1818`), non-finite logits raised rather than allowed to read as restraint (`:1774–1777`, mirrored at `:3397–3400`), empty density raised (`:1857`).
- **Three different masking mechanisms, each with different semantics** — attention causality by `-inf` fill (`:1700`), the schedule-only ablation by zeroing a feature slice (`:1741–1742`), and sequence padding by neither (zeros plus a `lengths` gather). Correctness of the third depends entirely on the conjunction of the causal mask (`:1726`) and the last-valid-index readout (`:1730`): padded positions are strictly after `lengths-1`, so causality makes them unreachable. Changing the readout to a mean-pool, or the mask to bidirectional, would silently ingest padded zeros as median-valued telemetry. `test_policy.py:31–39` pins exactly this property.
- **Factored objective mirrors the factored head**: WHICH trains at 1× from the full observed reward vector regardless of the gate, WHEN trains against a stop-gradiented mixture and only after a 30% warm-up (`:1903`, `:1959–1960`) — so the gate learns whether to trust a WHICH head that is already informative.
- Every symbol in the range carries `@semantic` (`:1681`, `:1707`, `:1736`, `:1746`, `:1753`, `:1785`, `:1801`, `:1810`, `:1839`, `:1855`, `:1873`, `:1879`, `:1910`, `:1927`), placing its source inside `config_hash()` (`:60–65`).
- Contrary to discovery finding §9, an external pytest suite for this subsystem does exist: `tests/unit/kernel_demo/test_policy.py` and `test_learning.py` (19 test files in that directory), covering shape/determinism, causality, parameter-count pinning, mask axis, warm-up boundary, stop-gradient, NOW-head gradient liveness, `SplitViolation` rejection, and synthetic-mapping learning on a true holdout.

**Concerns:**
- **The deployment rule is implemented twice and the tested copy is not the deployed copy.** `decide_live` (`:1754`) has zero production callers — its only caller anywhere in the repository is `tests/unit/kernel_demo/test_policy.py:64`. `run_eval` re-implements the same rule inline at `:3562–3573` using `_query_dicts` (`:3385`) and `_pi_argmax` (`:3416`). The author acknowledges the duplication and asks for manual lockstep ("the inline twin of the unit-tested `decide_live` … Keep the two in lockstep: `decide_live` is the tested owner", `:3568–3571`), but the arrangement inverts the guarantee: the function that produces every recorded eval decision has no direct unit test, and the function the tests pin produces nothing. The two copies already differ structurally — `decide_live` owns the window guard internally (`:1763`) while the deployed copy depends on the caller's `lo <= e <= hi` at `:3551`. A future edit to one is not mechanically detected by the other.
- **`record_to_vector` (`:397`) and `_telemetry_vector_from_dict` (`:1811`) are a twin contract enforced only by a comment.** `:1812` states "the field order MUST mirror it," but no test or assertion compares them. `test_telemetry.py:31–34` pins only `record_to_vector`'s shape and its element 0; `_telemetry_vector_from_dict` is imported by no test at all. This matters more than it looks: the dict twin feeds **every** live path (normalizer fitting `:2959`, training features `:1843`, eval queries `:3386`, gate 2 `:2588`), while `record_to_vector` survives only on `decide_live`'s dead path (`:1749`). A field-order drift between them would silently permute the feature space between what the normalizer was fit on and what a decision reads, with no failing test — and `decide_live`'s one test asserts only the window guard, never a value.
- **The `SEED_NAMES` index→name correspondence is load-bearing on the live path and pinned by nothing.** `seed_head` is `Linear(d, 4)` (`:1721`) and every consumer reads its output positionally as `SEED_NAMES[i]` — `decide_live`'s argmax (`:1781`), `query_teacher_forced`'s π dict (`:1797`), the training reward vector (`:1846`), `r_noop` (`:1851`), and both density gaps (`:1864`, `:1866`). No assertion or test establishes that correspondence: `test_policy.py:27` pins the head's *shape* as `(3, 4)` and nothing pins the *mapping*. Reordering the `SEED_NAMES` tuple (`:634`) would silently re-map every reward vector, every argmax and every reported per-seed statistic **with a fully green test suite**. This is the same defect class as the vectorizer twin above, but with an important difference in reachability: that twin is **latent** — `record_to_vector` reaches the model only through `_policy_tokens` (`:1747`), whose sole callers are the dead `decide_live` (`:1766`) and `query_teacher_forced` (`:1789`), so fit and inference cannot desynchronise today. The `SEED_NAMES` ordering has no such accidental protection; it is load-bearing **right now**, on the path that produces every recorded number. The trap is armed rather than merely present. The finding has two halves with very different remediation costs: the **arity** half — `Linear(d, 4)` is a bare literal with no `len(SEED_NAMES)` tie (`:1721`) — is a one-line fix, while the **correspondence** half needs a new assertion or test and is the more valuable of the two.
- **`query_teacher_forced` (`:1786`) is dead code.** It has zero callers in the repository. The teacher-forced agreement it was written to serve is actually computed by `_query_dicts(..., mask=False)` (`:3562`, `:3567`). Because both it and `decide_live` are `@semantic` (`:1753`, `:1785`), their source is inside `config_hash()` — so deleting or editing an unused function invalidates every recorded hash and causes `run_train` to refuse against an existing manifest (`:3343–3344`). Dead code on the semantic surface is therefore load-bearing dead code: costlier to remove than to keep, and the incentive runs toward keeping it.
- **An empty tune split silently disables checkpoint selection.** `train_policy` raises loudly when `train_ex` is empty (`:1947–1948`) but takes no position on `tune_ex`: if it is empty, `tune_batch` is `None` (`:1955`), the selection block never executes (`:1964`), `best_state` stays `None`, the `load_state_dict` at `:1973–1974` is skipped, and the **final-step** policy is returned. The only signal is an empty `"curve"` in the emitted JSON (`:1975`, `:3367`). The guard asymmetry means the run degrades from early-stopped to last-iterate without an exception.
- **A non-finite training loss is never checked, and the checkpoint selector fails open on the first evaluation.** The loop at `:1956–1963` has no gradient clipping, no LR schedule, and no `isfinite` guard on `loss`. `score < best_score` (`:1970`) is `False` for `NaN`, which is the right behaviour once a good checkpoint exists — but if the *first* tune evaluation is already `NaN`, `best_state` remains `None` and the final (possibly `NaN`) policy is returned and saved. Mitigation is real but downstream and indirect: any query against such a checkpoint raises at `:1777` or `:3400`, so the failure surfaces at eval time rather than silently, and the saved artifact's `state_hash` still identifies it.
- **`_telemetry_vector_from_dict` validates type and arity but never finiteness.** `float('nan')` and `float('inf')` are instances of `float` and pass `_as_float` (`:1805`) unchallenged. `TelemetryRecord` rejects non-finite values at construction (`:390–393`, pinned by `test_telemetry.py:18–22`), but a JSON-decoded dict bypasses that constructor entirely, and Python's `json.loads` accepts `NaN`/`Infinity` literals by default. A non-finite telemetry value round-tripped through the store therefore reaches the normalizer fit (`:2959`) and the token tensor (`:1843`). It would eventually surface via the non-finite logit guards, but after contaminating a frozen normalizer.
- The tune score is always computed with `enable_now=True` (`:1967`) while training steps below the warm-up threshold use `enable_now=False` (`:1959–1960`), so during the first 30% of steps the selection criterion includes a term the optimizer is not yet pursuing. Low practical risk — `best_score` starts at `+inf` and scores generally improve past warm-up — but the criterion and the objective are not the same function throughout the run.
- `schedule_only`'s ablation is "telemetry pinned to the normalizer median," not "telemetry absent": zeroing in normalized space (`:1741`) places every masked feature at a specific, learnable point rather than removing it. This is the correct construction given the fixed `TELEMETRY_DIM` input width and it does remove all per-episode variation, which is what the comparator claims — but the comparator's null is "a policy that sees only the clock and a constant," and that is the claim the reported margin supports.
